About

Power systems research translated into practical converter-control engineering.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at Virginia Tech. My work focuses on control and stability of power converters connected to AC and DC grids, with experience spanning grid-forming/grid-following control, microgrids, data-center power systems, power quality, EMT studies, and hardware-in-the-loop validation.

I have worked across research and industry environments, including power-electronics control engineering internships and real-time simulation of large electrical systems. I also build engineering automation tools in Python for analysis, compliance testing, and waveform post-processing.
